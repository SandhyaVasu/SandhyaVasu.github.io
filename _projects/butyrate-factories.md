The cross-feeding pair *Faecalibacterium prausnitzii* and *Bifidobacterium
adolescentis* drive efficient butyrate production between them. This tracks
their growth and butyrate secretion across diets, characterises their
interaction, and tests the Black Queen Hypothesis — then adds *Lactobacillus
brevis* as a third member and works through which orders of invasion let the
community thrive. Gluten-free and high-fibre diets came out best for butyrate.
